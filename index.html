Hi! I'm Oussama Enshassi.

I am an CAIT PhD candidate at the Data Mining Optimization (DMO) Lab at the Universiti Teknologi Malaysia. I am proud to be supervised by Prof. Dr. Masri Ayob. My Phd is part of the Operating rooms scheduling at Hospital Canselor Tuanku Muhriz (HCTM).
